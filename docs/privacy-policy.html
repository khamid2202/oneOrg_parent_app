Dombit Parents – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Dombit Parents ("the app") lets parents and guardians see their children's school information: schedule, attendance, grades, points, exam results, school calendar and notifications. The app is provided for schools that use the OneOrg / Dombit school management system. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Who can use the app

The app is intended for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. Accounts are not created in the app: the parent signs in with the phone number the school has on file for them, using a one-time code sent to it by SMS.

Data the app handles

  • Login details. Your phone number and the one-time SMS code. They are sent to the OneOrg server only to sign in and are not stored on the device. The school's server sends the code by SMS through its SMS provider.
  • Session token. After sign-in, the server issues a token. It is stored in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and is deleted when you log out.
  • School records of your children. Schedule, attendance, grades, points, rankings, exam results, calendar events and notifications. The app downloads this from the school's OneOrg server to show it to you. This data belongs to the school's records; the app does not create it.
  • App preferences. Your chosen language, theme and the last child you viewed, stored only on your device.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

How data is protected

All communication between the app and the server uses encrypted HTTPS connections. The session token is kept in the operating system's secure storage.

Retention and deletion

Logging out removes the session token and your saved data from the device. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on the device. School records are kept by the school according to its own policies; to have data about you or your child corrected or deleted, contact your school or email us at the address below.

Children

The app is used by parents, not by children. It shows information about students only to the parents or guardians the school has linked to them.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
